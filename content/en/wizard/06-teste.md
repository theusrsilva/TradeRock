With the room open as **Leader** on the Switch, TradeRock looks for the room and tries to
**decrypt** it, without joining. The result tells what's working:

- **Room found and decrypted:** radio and keys confirmed. Trading is unlocked.
- **Room found, but not decrypted:** the keys (prod.keys) aren't right.
- **No room found:** the Switch is too far, the room isn't open, or something is wrong with the
  radio. Bring the Switch closer, make sure it's the Leader and try again.
