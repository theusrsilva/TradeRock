Get the game ready on the Switch:

1. Play FireRed or LeafGreen until the **Direct Corner** is available (about 20 to 40 minutes).
2. Enter any **Pokémon Center** and go **upstairs**.
3. Talk to the **Direct Corner** attendant (left counter) and choose **trade**.
4. Choose to **be the Leader** (create the room). The Switch waits for someone to join.

When trading, the Switch shows a **join request**: that's TradeRock. Approve it, walk to the
**left chair** and follow the trade screen instructions.
