Plug the ESP32 in with a **data cable** and pick the port below. TradeRock checks that Linux lets
your user open the port and that nothing is "hijacking" the USB-serial converter.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- **Port group:** on Debian/Ubuntu serial ports belong to the `dialout` group; on Arch, to `uucp`.
  After joining the group, you must **log out and back in**.
- **brltty:** a braille display service that often "grabs" CH340/CP210x converters, making the port
  disappear. If you don't use a braille display, you can disable it.
- **udev rule:** optional; gives the port a fixed name (`/dev/frlg-esp32`), handy when the port
  changes name after replugging the board.

</details>
