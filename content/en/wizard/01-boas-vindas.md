**TradeRock** makes your PC (or a small ESP32 board) **pretend to be a second Switch** in the
FireRed and LeafGreen trade room. That way you send Pokémon from your PC to your game and keep the
ones you receive.

This assistant checks, step by step, that everything is ready. It takes about **10 to 20 minutes**,
not counting buying a part, if you need one.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

The Switch game uses the console's "local wireless" (LDN) in the Direct Corner trade room.
TradeRock speaks that same protocol: it joins the room your Switch created, sits in the other chair
and trades as if it were another player.
