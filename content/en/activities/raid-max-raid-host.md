**On Sword/Shield:**

1. Click **Start the raid** here.
2. Press **Y** to open **Y-Comm** in local wireless mode and pick **POKELDN**'s raid from the list.
3. Press **Ready**: the console runs the battle and TradeRock's Pokémon fights at your side.
4. Win the raid to get the rewards. When the battle ends, click **Done** here.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- **Over your home network (LAN mode):** in Options, press **L + R + left stick** together. In Y-Comm, press **X** to refresh, pick POKELDN's raid, then **Join** and **Start Battle**.
- The boss must be a species that shows up in the game's dens. Otherwise the console leaves at Ready.
- The rewards are the den's (or the chosen event's).

</details>
