**On Sword/Shield:**

1. Open the menu (**X**), choose **Mystery Gift** and the option to receive a gift through **local wireless**, not the internet.
2. Click **Send** here: the card shows up on the console within a few seconds, or not at all.
3. Accept the card on the console, then click **Done** here.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- The console only "hears" the gift and doesn't tell the computer it received it. That's why you confirm it: if the card arrived, it worked.
- The gift stays on the air for 5 minutes, or until you click **Done**.
- The game may refuse a card the save already received.

</details>
