**On FireRed/LeafGreen:**

1. On the title screen, choose **MYSTERY GIFT**, then **WONDER CARDS** and **FRIEND**. For news, the second entry, **WONDER NEWS**.
2. Click **Send** here and pick **POKELDN** when it shows up on the console.
3. If the console asks whether to replace the card it has, answer **YES**.
4. To get the gift, save the game and talk to the **delivery man** on 2F of any Pokémon Center.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- If **MYSTERY GIFT** isn't on the title screen, answer **LINK TOGETHER WITH ALL** in the questionnaire of any Poké Mart.
- Between two sends, back out of the console's search screen.
- The game keeps one Wonder Card at a time: a new gift replaces the previous one.

</details>
