**On Scarlet/Violet:**

1. Press **X**, open the **Poké Portal**, choose **Union Circle** and start a **local** group with a 4-digit code.
2. Enter the same code here, choose the Pokémon and click **Join the circle**.
3. Once **POKELDN** is in the group, start the circle on the console. As its start demo ends, TradeRock's player follows yours.
4. Three of the Pokémon come 15 to 25 m around you. One you battle, or walk more than 50 m from, comes back near you.
5. To stop, close the circle on the console.
