**On Scarlet/Violet:**

1. Click **Start the raid** here.
2. Press **X**, open the **Poké Portal**, choose **Tera Raid Battle**, search **offline** and use code **4970**.
3. Press **Ready** on each console.
4. When the battle starts, TradeRock leaves and its Pokémon keeps fighting. If a communication error shows up, dismiss it and fight on.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- The boss, its Tera type and the rewards come from the seed, the version and the story stage.
- Each console fights its own battle: TradeRock doesn't know who won.

</details>
