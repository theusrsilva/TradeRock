**On Scarlet/Violet:**

1. At a Tera Raid crystal, choose **Challenge as a group** and wait for players.
2. Click **Start the raid** here: TradeRock joins the room.
3. Start the battle on the console. TradeRock's Pokémon fights as a partner.
