The data of **your** Switch save. TradeRock uses it as the original trainer of the Pokémon you create
and to warn about obedience and the National Pokédex.

- **Name** and **ID (TID)** are on the in-game trainer card.
- The **secret ID (SID)** isn't shown in the game. To find it: send the PC, in a trade, a Pokémon
  **you** caught, then use **"Take from a received Pokémon"**.
- With your trainer, the Pokémon arrives as if it were yours: it always obeys and can be nicknamed.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

In FireRed/LeafGreen, a Pokémon from another trainer disobeys above level 10, 30 (2nd badge), 50 (4th)
or 70 (6th); with the 8th badge it always obeys. Without the National Pokédex, the game won't trade
Pokémon above #151 or Eggs. Rules checked in the game's code (pret/pokefirered).
