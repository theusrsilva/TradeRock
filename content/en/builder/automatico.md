Pick the Pokémon and what you want from it. TradeRock builds a **legal** version, as if it had
really been caught, and shows right away whether it worked.

- Leave **"any"** for what doesn't matter: a possible combination is easier to find.
- If something you asked for isn't possible, TradeRock shows **what changed** and why.
- The origin is picked automatically, preferring FireRed/LeafGreen. You can pick another under **Origin**.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

In the third generation, nature, ability, gender and shininess aren't chosen separately: they all
come from a number called the **PID**, and the IVs come from the same RNG call. The game only produces
certain combinations and PKHeX checks that link ("PID-IV correlation", Method 1/2/4). That's why
shiny, a specific nature and perfect IVs sometimes can't all happen in the same encounter.

AutoMod (PKHeX-Plugins) generates; PKHeX's LegalityAnalysis checks.
