**In Legends: Z-A:**

1. Press **X** and open **Link Play** → **Link Trade** → **Nearby Players**.
2. Use the **same eight digits** you set here and start searching.
3. Choose the Pokémon you are sending and confirm.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- Without a code, use 00000000.
- **Do not stop in the middle of a trade:** the game shows an error and cancels the trade.
- It works the same on Switch and Switch 2 (Switch 2 Edition).
- If a Pokémon evolves by trade, the Switch evolves the one it receives.

</details>
