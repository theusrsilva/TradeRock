**In FireRed/LeafGreen (with the ESP32):**

1. Go to the **second floor of a Pokémon Center** and talk to the **third attendant** (Direct Corner).
2. Choose **Trade Center**, then **Join Group**. Do not create the group: TradeRock creates it.
3. Pick from the list the trainer name you set here in TradeRock.
4. Sit down, choose the Pokémon you are sending and confirm.
5. After the last trade, wait for the game to save, then choose **Cancel** and **Yes** to leave.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- With several trades, trade again after each save: TradeRock offers the next Pokémon.
- If a Pokémon evolves by trade, the Switch evolves the one it receives.

</details>
