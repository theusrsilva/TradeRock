**In Let's Go:**

1. Press **X** and open **Communicate** → **Local Communication** → **Trade**.
2. Pick the **same three Pokémon** as the code you set here, in the same order.
3. Search and wait for TradeRock to show up.
4. Choose the Pokémon you are sending and confirm.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- Without a code, use three Pikachu (the default code).
- **Do not stop in the middle of a trade:** the game locks trading for about 10 minutes of play.
- If a Pokémon evolves by trade, the Switch evolves the one it receives.

</details>
