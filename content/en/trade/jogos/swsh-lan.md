<!-- Sword/Shield LAN mode (D24). Steps from pokeldn (catalog.py, swsh-lan-host), tested on a real console. -->
**In Sword/Shield, over your home network:**

1. Make sure the Switch and this computer are on the **same network**.
2. If LAN mode is not on yet: in **Options**, press **L + R + the left stick** together. The game says LAN mode is on.
3. Press **Y** to open **Y-Comm** and choose **Link Trade**. A link code, if you set one, is ignored.
4. Press **A** on both messages: TradeRock shows up on the trade screen.
5. Choose the Pokémon you are sending and confirm.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- The game warns that only Max Raid Battles are guaranteed in LAN mode. Trading worked with a real Sword, on a Switch 2 over Wi-Fi.
- On Windows, the installer opens the trade ports only on "Private" networks. If your home network is marked "Public", the Switch will not find TradeRock.
- To turn LAN mode off, repeat the same command in Options.

</details>
