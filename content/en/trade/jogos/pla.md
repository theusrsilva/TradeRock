**In Legends: Arceus:**

1. In **Jubilife Village**, talk to the person who handles trades.
2. Choose to trade with someone **nearby** (local) and go past the warning.
3. Enter the **same eight digits** you set here and press **+** to search.
4. Choose the Pokémon you are sending and confirm.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- Without a code, use 00000000.
- **Do not stop in the middle of a trade:** the game locks trading for about 10 minutes.
- If a Pokémon evolves by trade, the Switch evolves the one it receives.

</details>
