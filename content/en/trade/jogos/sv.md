**In Scarlet/Violet:**

1. Press **X** and open the **Poké Portal**.
2. Choose **Link Trade** and play **offline** (local trade).
3. Use the **same code** you set here (or none, if you left it blank) and start searching.
4. Choose the Pokémon you are sending and confirm.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

- Do not turn off or leave in the middle of a trade.
- If a Pokémon evolves by trade, the Switch evolves the one it receives.

</details>
