Choose the **game** and the **trainer** the PC will pretend to be.

- The name shows up on the Switch in the request to join the room. **Remember it**: that's the one you'll approve.
- Use up to **7 characters**: letters without accents, digits and a few symbols (`. - ! ?`).
- ID and secret ID are optional. Save combinations you always use as **saved trainers**.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

The simulated trainer's ID (TID) and secret ID (SID) go on the trainer card exchanged in the
room. The engine default is trainer "EMU" (TID 34850, SID 18413).

</details>
