Build the **PC party**: the Pokémon TradeRock pretends to have, as if it were a second Switch.

- Mark which ones go **to your Switch**. You can do 1 to 6 trades in a row.
- The party needs **at least 2 Pokémon**. The game never lets anyone trade away their last Pokémon, so one always stays behind as "filler".
- Every Pokémon used in a trade is stored in TradeRock before it starts.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

Pokémon are third-generation `.pk3` (or `.ek3`) files. TradeRock builds the party the Switch
sees in the trade room from them. When trading, you pick on the Switch which of your Pokémon
goes to the PC; from the PC side goes the Pokémon marked for that trade.

</details>
