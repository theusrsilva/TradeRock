Instructions shown during the trade, one section per phase (the "## key" heading is not shown).
Variables: {trainer} = simulated trainer name, {n} = trade number, {total} = number of trades.
-->

## procurando
Looking for your Switch's room. Keep the Direct Corner open as **Leader**.

## tentando_de_novo
The connection didn't work this time. TradeRock will **try again** on its own. Keep the room open on the Switch.

## aprovar
A request to join the room showed up on the Switch. **Approve {trainer}'s request.**

## andar_cadeira
Connected! On the Switch, **walk to the LEFT chair** at the trade table and sit down. Walking may be a bit laggy.

## aguarde
All good. Wait while both sides exchange trainer cards.

## escolher
On the Switch, **choose the Pokémon** you want to send to the PC for trade {n} of {total}.

## confirmar
**Confirm the trade** on the Switch.

## proxima
Trade {n} of {total} done! On the Switch, choose the Pokémon for the **next trade**.

## sair
Trades done! On the Switch, **cancel** the trade menu and **leave the room** through the door.

## cancelado_switch
The trade was cancelled on the Switch. **Leave the room** on the Switch.

## fim
Done! The connection was closed.
