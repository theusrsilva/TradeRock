Before starting, get the Switch ready:

1. In FireRed or LeafGreen, go to any **Pokémon Center** and go **upstairs**.
2. Talk to the **Direct Corner** attendant (left counter) and choose **trade**.
3. Choose to **be the Leader** (create the room). The Switch waits for someone to join.
4. Keep the Switch close to the PC (or the ESP32).

Once the Switch is waiting, click **Start trade**.

<details><summary>Learn more</summary>

The Direct Corner unlocks after about 20 to 40 minutes of play. The Switch creates a local
wireless network and TradeRock joins it as if it were a second Switch. The connection may take
a few attempts: TradeRock retries on its own.

</details>
